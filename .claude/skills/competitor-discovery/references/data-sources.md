## Search angles

Rotate these query shapes (swap the year for the current one):

- "mining AI startup funding <year>"
- "mineral exploration AI company seed / Series A"
- "critical minerals discovery AI startup"
- "core scanning / geophysics / subsurface AI company"
- "<accelerator/VC name> mining portfolio company"

## Scope filter (what counts)

In scope: mineral exploration AI, geoscience/ML, core scanning, subsurface /
geophysics AI, critical-minerals discovery, mining software/analytics.

Out of scope: pure mining operators with no AI/software angle, generic
geospatial tools with no mining focus, hardware-only vendors unless explicitly
AI-driven.
